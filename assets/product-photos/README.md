# Product photo edits

The six original 194 × 252 JPEG photographs were supplied by the user. They are preserved byte-for-byte in `originals/`; `sources.json` records their SHA-256 hashes.

The built-in image-editing tool was used for restrained photo correction. Its edited PNG copies are in `enhanced/`. Website WebP assets are in `public/images/products/`, limited to 388 × 504 pixels, with 194 × 252 alternatives. No CSS filters are used. The full enhanced photograph opens in the lightbox without a crop. Only the treats section uses these assets; hero and about imagery are unchanged.

Regenerate the optimized files with `node scripts/prepare-product-assets.mjs`. Product names, descriptions, image paths, alt text, and crop positions are editable in `src/data/products.js`.
